What's new in IngeTrazo

The latest two releases: 0.6.3, devoted to groups and components, and 0.6.2, devoted to sheets and dimensioning. The full notes, with every change and its author, are in the GitHub releases.

0.6.3 · Groups and components

November 2026. More than 30 people contributed code to this release: thank you all.

Groups and components

  • Component copies follow your changes: moving or scaling a group inside a component reaches every copy
  • Drawing inside an assembly reaches its copies, each in its own place
  • Nested components when importing .skp: complex models no longer melt into a single component
  • My library: your own component folders, as a tree, and the component tree in the sidebar
  • Scale manager with linked XYZ and a choice of pivot
  • Editing inside a group no longer leaves the ~0.4 s stutter that followed every edit

Smoother, with the Rust engine

  • Move, Rotate and Scale leave the model alone while you drag: it changes once, when you let go, with the same undo and exact typed values
  • 6,400 faces of loose geometry: from ~320–350 ms to 1.3–3 ms per frame
  • Scaling a legacy group: from 87 to 1.3 ms
  • Push/Pull on a slab with holes: from 51 to 5 ms per frame
  • Snapping to lines: from ~31 to ~6 ms per move
  • Switching sheets with a large model: from ~2 s to ~0.1 s

Measured on our test models; on your computer and with your model the figures will differ.

Sheets

  • Sheet tabs you reorder by dragging, with undo
  • “Sheets” panel with a thumbnail of each one: select, reorder, rename and delete
  • Editable legend: a name and colour of your own per layer, plus free entries
  • Backdrop for the scale bar, the north arrow and the legend, readable over an orthophoto
  • Sheet index and sheet browser

Drawing and modelling

  • Section box: clip the model with a six-sided box, in the view and on sheets
  • Draw ▸ Terrain: create, sculpt, stamp and drape editable terrain, also from the survey points of a DXF
  • Cut opening: a window or door cuts its opening in the wall, even when the wall is a group, and the opening follows it when moved
  • Make faces and Extrude: each outline becomes its own solid in one step
  • Calculator in the measurements box: type =1.25*2+2.45/2 where a length goes
  • Guide Point tool, inferences you switch on one by one, and a Tape measure that rescales the model

View, camera and presentation

  • Camera from photo: find the camera that took a photo so the model matches it
  • Composition guides and Hide scenes (Ctrl+E)
  • Full style editor, horizon gradient, fog, and opacity in layer and material colours
  • Export scenes as a PNG sequence, with an optional transparent background
  • Trackpad: pinch zooms towards your fingers; 3D mouse with camera roll, and SpaceMouse on macOS

BIM and IFC

  • BIM table: search, filter by IFC class, group and see the path in the tree, with totals of quantities and volume
  • Stable IFC4.3 export and verified delivery packages (the first profile, for Türkiye)
  • IFC in world coordinates, with a Reference View header, units, project north and site location
  • Location tab in Model Info, and the table of materials in use to CSV

AI and automation

  • Named MCP tools, with no arbitrary code and all with undo
  • Server mode: ingetrazo --server [file] [--port N]
  • Window-less: ingetrazo --headless --ai-bridge --open House.igz, with save and shutdown from MCP
  • Save as plain JSON for version control
  • Each open document is addressed separately across windows

Materials, extensions, languages

  • Export and import .igmat materials, “My materials”, and import .skm materials
  • Texture colour tint that keeps the bright and dull parts
  • Improved Extension Manager and a resource library with .iglib bundles
  • Turkish and Catalan in the interface, plus Vietnamese terms
  • Windows: .igz thumbnails in Explorer, recent files in the taskbar, and publishing on Winget

0.6.2 · Sheets and dimensioning

14 November 2026, with the 0.6.2.1 fix released the same night.

Sheets and dimensions

  • Sheet dimensions that snap along edges, with the label below or flipped and offset from the text
  • Cut weight and hatch per layer, on sheets, in DXF and in the 3D view
  • Extensions draw on sheets too: the stationing of a road or a canal comes out as vectors
  • Export every sheet to PDF from the command line, with no window
  • The composer for a test plaza opens in 2.5 s instead of 57 s

Scenes, camera and video

  • Scene transitions, Play, and exporting the walkthrough as video
  • Scenes with folders and thumbnails in their own tab
  • North tool and Camera ▸ Camera settings: focal length, sensor and lens shift
  • Several viewports, each with its own style, projection and scene
  • Align view: the camera faces the chosen face square on

Interface and export

  • Four new themes —Studio, Solarized, Campbell and Sand—, an improved Dark theme and thinner icons
  • Export the view as layered SVG, with faces, textures and sun shadows
  • Soft shadows, analytic guide circles and a “Face centre” snap
  • Push/Pull several faces at once, each along its own normal

Huge models

  • A 21,000-group model: first frame from 60 s to 21 s, memory from 9 GB to 5.4 GB
  • A 1,035-object plaza exports its plan as SVG in 17 s instead of 170
  • 0.6.2.1: the sidebar no longer freezes the window with large models (from 12–22 s to a tenth of a second)
  • Dozens of fixes: Ctrl+S with extensions open, exact copies with Move+Ctrl, and the Mac crash when importing a .skp with scenes
